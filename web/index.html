Integrity stamp + vault

Every signing generates a random AES-256 key. The signed PDF is encrypted into a .vault file; the QR carries the key and (if uploaded) the vault URL. Anyone who scans the QR can fetch the vault, decrypt back to the exact signed PDF and verify it — no server logic of yours involved.
