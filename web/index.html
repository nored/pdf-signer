On sign, your identity's certificate is auto-published to <vault>/certs/<fingerprint>.pem once, and each signed PDF carries the raw URL. Verifiers auto-fetch it and trust the signer transitively via the vault's HTTPS chain — no manual trust setup on the recipient's side. Trusted roots

Roots the verifier trusts when checking cert chains. Automatically populated: creating an identity self-trusts its own cert, importing a p12 with a chain trusts the chain's root, and clicking "Trust current identity's root" below trusts whatever's active. Manual PEM paste is only needed for third-party CAs (a colleague's cert, an experimental root).

Trusted timestamp (RFC 3161)

Default rfc3161.ai.moda is one of the few public TSAs that sends CORS headers, so it works from a browser. Most other public TSAs (freeTSA, DigiCert, Sectigo) reject browser fetches; a failure toast will name the URL that refused. Adobe silent-green requires a TSA on Adobe's own trust list — none of the free ones qualify.
